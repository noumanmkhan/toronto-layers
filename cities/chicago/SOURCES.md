# Raw data: where each file in `raw/` came from

Everything here is fetched in GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/fetch-chicago.yml`), because the
sandbox these maps are built in can't reach the source servers. Fetched on 2026-10-07 unless noted.

| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `community_areas.geojson` | City of Chicago Data Portal, Boundaries - Community Areas (`igwz-8jzy`), the 77 community areas |
| `neighborhoods.geojson` | City of Chicago Data Portal, Boundaries - Neighborhoods (`y6yq-dbs2`, 98 areas, 2012). Used only for the known-as names that aren't community areas |
| `skyscrapers/wiki.json` | English Wikipedia, "List of tallest buildings in Chicago" (CC BY-SA 4.0): every table on the page with its section, cell text and article links, each linked article's coordinates and Wikidata id (Wikipedia API), and the street addresses in `skyscrapers.json` geocoded with OSM Nominatim. Fetched by `.github/workflows/fetch-skyscrapers.yml` running `engine/fetch_skyscrapers.py` (monthly, or when the script or `skyscrapers.json` changes). `engine/skyscrapers.py` builds `skyscrapers.geojson` from it and `skyscrapers.json` (towers left out, which of this year's towers are open, hand-placed coordinates, former names, curated facts). |
| `institutions/ipeds_hd.csv` | IPEDS institutional characteristics, U.S. Department of Education (NCES), HD2024 directory file, Illinois rows: control, level, degree-granting, size category, coordinates. Used by `institutions.py` for the Universities and City Colleges toggles. |
| `institutions/cms_hospitals.csv`, `cms_geocoded.json` | CMS Hospital General Information (data.cms.gov provider data `xubh-q36u`), Chicago rows: ownership and emergency services, for the ERs toggle; addresses placed by the Census Bureau geocoder (kept for reference; positions come from OSM). |
| `institutions/osm.json` | OpenStreetMap (ODbL) via Overpass: universities, colleges and hospitals in Chicago, plus DePaul, Loyola, Northwestern and Rush buildings by name, for campus and hospital positions. All four fetched 2026-10-10 by `fetch_institutions.py` (the *Fetch Chicago data* workflow). |
| `nearby/cpl_branches.json`, `nearby/osm_amenities.json` | Chicago Public Library branches (City Data Portal `x8fc-8rcq`) and, from OpenStreetMap via Overpass, public parks, playgrounds and tennis courts in the city (community centres no longer come from OSM), fetched 2026-10-10 by `fetch_nearby.py`. `nearby.py` builds `docs/chicago/data/nearby.json` for the card's Nearby section. Pools and rinks are fetched but not used: OSM names too few of the Park District's pools for a count to be right. |
| `nearby/centres/fieldhouses.json`, `dfss_community_service_centers.json`, `cpd_facilities.json`, `dfss_senior_centers.json` | Community centres for the card, fetched 2026-10-10 by `fetch_centres.py`. Rule: publicly run and open to everyone. Chicago Park District's current fieldhouse list (chicagoparkdistrict.com/facilities/fieldhouses, 230 entries with addresses), placed by the U.S. Census Bureau's batch geocoder; `nearby.py` keeps a geocode only when house number, direction and ZIP agree (it settled for N. Washtenaw for S. Washtenaw, Wolcott for Olcott), otherwise uses the same-named park in OpenStreetMap. Beach houses, visitor and nature centres left out. Plus the City's six DFSS Community Service Centers (Data Portal `bspy-6mw8`, 2016; unchanged since). Senior centers (`qhfc-4cw2`) are fetched but not used, since they serve 60+ only; CPD facilities (`eix4-gf83`, 2016) is kept for reference. |
| `wards.geojson` | City of Chicago Data Portal, Boundaries - Wards (2023-) (`p293-wvbd`) |
| `school_board_20.zip`, `school_board_20.geojson` | Chicago Board of Education subdistricts enacted by Public Act 103-0584 (SB 15, Senate Floor Amendment 1, signed March 18, 2024): `ERSB_20_Sub_District_Map_FA1_SB_15.zip` from the Illinois Senate Redistricting Committee (ilsenateredistricting.com), fetched 2026-10-10 by `fetch_school_board.py`. The GeoJSON is the same shapefile converted with mapshaper. These 20 districts (1a … 10b) are on the November 3, 2026 ballot. Not on the City's Data Portal. |
| `school_board_10.zip` | The act's 10-district map (used in 2024), same source. Kept as a check: each district is exactly its two subdistricts (differences under 0.003% of area). |
| `school_board/cboe_candidate_list.pdf`, `cboe_ballot_lottery.pdf` | Chicago Board of Elections, "Candidate List" for the November 3, 2026 General Election (status as of September 11, 2026) and the ballot-placement lottery results, linked from chicagoelections.gov/getting-ballot/candidates. `school_board_candidates.py` (run by hand; needs pypdf) reads the Board of Education contests into `school_board/candidates.json`. |
| `school_board/results.json` | Board of Education results from chicagoelections.gov/elections/results, fetched by `fetch_school_board_results.py` (the representatives job, hourly on election night and daily until the results are proclaimed). The page loads each contest with a Drupal AJAX post; the script does the same. Absent until the board posts the 2026 general's results page. `results_probe.json` is the 2024 general's school board contests, kept to test the parser. |
| `attendance/elementary.geojson`, `middle.geojson`, `high.geojson`, `profiles.json`, `sources.json` | City of Chicago Data Portal, Chicago Public Schools attendance boundaries for school year 2025–26: elementary (`x72b-38qv`, 356 areas), middle (`fyff-53xy`, 22 schools) and high school (`xg7c-d8rm`, 49 areas); and CPS School Profile Information SY2024–25 (`3dhs-m3w4`, the newest published) for each school's full name, building location and profile link. Fetched 2026-10-10 by `fetch_attendance.py`, which picks the newest school year in the portal's catalog, so re-running it after CPS publishes SY2026–27 picks that up. `attendance.py` builds `docs/chicago/data/attendance.geojson`. Ratings, enrolment and demographics in the profile file are not used. |
| `ssa.geojson` | City of Chicago Data Portal, Boundaries - Special Service Areas (`cmr6-dn8c`); active SSAs only |
| `heritage_districts.geojson`, `landmark_districts.json` | City of Chicago Data Portal, Boundaries - Landmark Districts (`t8pq-wu86`, a shapefile last updated in 2012, so districts designated since then are missing) and the Landmark Districts list (`zidz-sdfj`), fetched 2026-10-07 by `.github/workflows/fetch-heritage.yml`. `heritage.py` drops a trailing "District" from names and writes out two names cut short in the shapefile |
| `city.geojson` | City of Chicago Data Portal, Boundaries - City (`qqq8-j68g`) |
| `cta_lines.geojson` | City of Chicago Data Portal, CTA - 'L' (Rail) Lines (`xbyr-jnvx`): track segments, each listing the lines that use it |
| `cta_stops.json` | City of Chicago Data Portal, CTA - System Information - List of 'L' Stops (`8pix-ypme`): platforms grouped into stations by `map_id` |
| `ward_offices.json`, `acs_community_areas.json` | City of Chicago Data Portal, Ward Offices (`htai-wnw4`) and ACS 5 Year Data by Community Area (`t68z-cikk`). The second has no housing or commuting figures, so the lens uses the tract tables below instead |
| `places.geojson`, `counties.geojson` | U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb: Incorporated Places and Counties in Illinois and Indiana around Chicagoland. Used for the neighbouring municipalities and their counties |
| `congress.geojson`, `il_senate.geojson`, `il_house.geojson` | U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb, Legislative service: 120th Congressional Districts and 2026 State Legislative Districts (upper and lower), Illinois |
| `lake_michigan.geojson` | U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb, Areal Hydrography: the Lake Michigan tiles along Chicagoland's shore (fetched by `fetch_lake.py`). Enclosed harbours aren't in them; `build.py` treats lakefront land no municipality claims as harbour water |
| `lakes.geojson` | Natural Earth 10m lakes: Lake Michigan, used away from Chicagoland's shore |
| `osm_expressways.json` | OpenStreetMap (ODbL) via the Overpass API: `highway=motorway` and `trunk` ways across Chicagoland |
| `osm_arterials.json` | OpenStreetMap (ODbL) via the Overpass API: named `primary`, `secondary` and `tertiary` roads inside the city (tertiary roads are drawn on the Collector streets layer by `collectors.py`) |
| `metra.json` | Metra's GTFS feed (`schedules.metrarail.com/gtfs/schedule.zip`), reduced by `fetch_extra.py` to each line's busiest outbound patterns, its stations and the lines stopping at each |
| `acs_tracts.json` | U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020-2024 5-year table-based summary files (`www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2024/table-based-SF/`): B25003 tenure, B08301 means of transportation to work, B25075 home value and B25063 gross rent, for every Cook County tract. The Census API now requires a key from GitHub's runners; the bulk files don't |
| `acs_more.json` | Same ACS 2020-2024 summary files, fetched by `fetch_acs_more.py`: B25036 year structure built and B25032 units in structure (occupied homes), C16001 language spoken at home (people 5 and over, 12 broad groups; the detailed B16001 isn't published for tracts), B01001 sex by age and B05005 period of entry (whose universe is people born outside the US), with their variable labels from the Census API's metadata. Summed into community areas by `profiles.py` for the card's Home types, Built, Language at home, Ages and Recent immigrants rows and the housing-type lens |
| `tracts.json` | U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb: each Cook County tract's internal point, used to place it in a community area |
| `point_check.json` | OpenStreetMap Nominatim positions for the curated landmarks and added known-as names (`fetch_check.py`), to compare against the lists in `landmarks.py` and `names.py` |
| `representatives/` | Fetched by `fetch_representatives.py`: `ward_offices.json` (City Data Portal, alderpersons and ward websites), `il.csv` (Open States, current Illinois legislators), `house.xml` (Clerk of the U.S. House member list). `representatives.py` matches them to the map's wards and districts |
| `discovery/` | One-off listing of candidate sources (`discover.py`), kept as a record of what was available |
| `transit_loop.json` | Computed by `fetch_transit_loop.py` (the *Compute transit times to the Loop* GitHub Action) with r5py: weekday 8–9 am trips from about 3,700 points across the 77 community areas to the quickest of five Loop arrival points. Inputs: CTA GTFS (`transitchicago.com/downloads/sch_data/google_transit.zip`), Metra GTFS, the South Shore Line's GTFS (NICTD, `mysouthshoreline.com/google/google_transit.zip`, with the Mobility Database's copy as a fallback) and OpenStreetMap for walking (BBBike's Chicago extract, or Geofabrik's Illinois extract cut to the city). Both feeds are tidied for R5 (Metra's padding trimmed, the CTA's empty frequencies.txt left out). The exact URLs, the departure date and which Loop point each trip ended at are recorded inside the file; `transit_loop.log` is the run's log. Fetched on 2026-10-09. |
| `drive_points.json`, `drive_times.json` | The suburban lens: `engine/drive.py points chicago` lays a grid about 1.5 km apart over the municipalities around the city; `engine/fetch_drive_times.py` (the *Fetch drive times* GitHub Action) times each point by car to State & Madison with OSRM's public server on OpenStreetMap roads, free-flow, and drops points more than 1 km from a road. Fetched on 2026-10-07. |
| `construction/osm.json` | OpenStreetMap (ODbL) via the Overpass API: rail lines, stations and routes tagged as under construction in the regional box. Fetched by `.github/workflows/fetch-construction.yml` (monthly, or when the script changes) running `engine/fetch_construction.py`; `inventory.txt` lists what came back. `engine/construction.py` keeps only the projects in `construction.json`. |
| `closures/permits.json` | CDOT's Transportation Department Permits (Chicago Data Portal, Socrata `pubx-yq2d`, updated daily): open permits active now or within a week whose name, comments or detail name the Red Line Extension ("RLE") or Red and Purple Modernization ("RPM") as whole words, with a Full, Partial or Curblane street closure. The far end of each address range is placed with the address grid (800 numbers a mile). Fetched daily just after midnight Eastern by `.github/workflows/fetch-closures.yml` running `fetch_closures.py`, which then runs `engine/closures.py` to write `docs/chicago/data/closures.geojson`. The log is `closures/fetch.log`. |
| `surface/routes.json`, `surface/stops.json` | Fetched by `engine/fetch_surface.py` (the *Fetch streetcar and bus routes* GitHub Action, monthly) from the CTA GTFS (`transitchicago.com/downloads/sch_data/google_transit.zip`), source in `cities/chicago/surface.json`. Streetcar and bus routes only (route numbers in `skip` are drawn by the rapid-transit layer): the shapes carrying at least 10% of a direction's trips over the coming week, simplified to about 5 m; trips per hour of first departure on one weekday, one Saturday and one Sunday; every stop with the routes serving it that week. The feed URL, the week used and the fetch date are recorded in the files; `surface/fetch.log` is the run's log. Built into the map by `engine/surface.py`. |

Hand-kept lists, in code: the nine sides, the known-as additions and the major streets (`names.py`), and the landmarks (`landmarks.py`).
